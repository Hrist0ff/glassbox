Bike shop sales report, 2025

Units sold per quarter:
Q1: 120 bikes
Q2: 310 bikes
Q3: 290 bikes
Q4: no figures were recorded because the point-of-sale system was being replaced.

Electric bikes were part of these totals: 20 in Q1, 75 in Q2, and 95 in Q3.

The owner believes the spring jump came from a new cycling path that opened in April, but no survey was done to confirm this.
