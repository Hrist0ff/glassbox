Incident review: checkout errors on 12 June

Summary: For 41 minutes, about 30% of checkout requests failed with server errors. No payments were charged twice.

Timeline (all times UTC)
09:02 The payments team deployed version 4.18 of the checkout service.
09:15 The primary database for orders reached its connection limit.
09:16 Error rates on checkout rose above 25%.
09:18 The on-call engineer was paged automatically.
09:24 The support team posted a status page notice.
09:31 The payments team rolled back to version 4.17. Errors continued.
09:40 The database team found a reporting job, started at 09:10, holding about 400 idle connections.
09:43 The reporting job was stopped and its connections were released.
09:57 Error rates returned to normal.

Cause: The reporting job opened a new connection for each query and never closed them, so the orders database ran out of connections. The deploy at 09:02 was not related; rolling it back had no effect.

Follow-up: Limit connections per job. At some point the reporting job had been moved to run in the morning instead of at night; the review did not find out when or why.
