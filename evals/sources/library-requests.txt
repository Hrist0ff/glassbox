How book requests work at the Eastside branch library

1. A member asks for a book that is not on the shelf, at the desk or in the app.
2. The system checks the other branches. If a copy is available, a transfer is requested.
3. Transfers travel on the delivery van, which visits every branch on Tuesdays and Fridays.
4. When the book arrives, it is placed on the holds shelf and the member gets a text message.
5. The member has 7 days to pick it up. After that, the book goes to the next person waiting, or back to its home branch.
